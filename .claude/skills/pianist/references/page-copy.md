# Homepage Copy (rewritten to the copy rules)

**Source copy (the brief):** "Srinijakara: A personal portfolio and music website for a passionate pianist, showcasing her performances, musical journey, repertoire, and upcoming work."

**What changed:** "passionate", "musical journey" and "showcasing" are banned words, so they're gone. The four ideas in that sentence (performances, journey, repertoire, upcoming work) each get their own screen. Every claim is a concrete fact. Facts we don't have yet are in `[brackets]`; fill them with real details and never leave invented ones in.

| Brief phrase | Becomes |
|---|---|
| "a passionate pianist" | The name, large, and a dated next concert |
| "showcasing her performances" | "Hear her live" + concert rows; one filmed performance |
| "musical journey" | About screen: where she trained, where she plays now (facts, third person) |
| "repertoire" | "What she plays": composers and works, counted |
| "upcoming work" | "Next season": one named project with a date |
